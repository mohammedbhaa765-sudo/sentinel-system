Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the Sentinel System Discord bot (“the Bot”) handles, why, and what choices you have.

In short: the Bot stores only what it needs to run moderation features (settings and moderation records). It does not sell data, does not read private messages, and does not store the content of ordinary server messages.

1. Information we handle

The Bot does not collect passwords, payment details, email addresses, IP addresses or the content of direct messages sent to it.

2. Logs inside your server

If a server enables logging, the Bot posts events into the log and alert channels chosen by that server’s administrators. These can include edited or deleted message content, honeypot triggers, member joins and leaves, role changes and voice activity. These posts are stored by Discord inside that server and are controlled by its administrators, not by us.

3. Direct messages

The Bot may send a direct message to a member to tell them about an action taken against them (for example a ban, kick, timeout or jail) and the reason. The Bot does not read replies sent to it in direct messages.

4. How we use information

Only to provide the features described above, keep the Bot secure and fix problems. We do not use data for advertising or profiling.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent data. Data is processed through Discord’s platform, and the Bot runs on third-party hosting (Wispbyte) where its database is stored. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Settings and moderation records are kept while the Bot is in your server.
  • Jail records are removed when the member is released.
  • You can ask us to delete the data for your server or for your own user ID (see Contact). Server administrators can also remove blocked words, trusted users and honeypots with the Bot’s commands, and can clear warnings with /clearwarns.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask what moderation records the Bot holds about your user ID, ask for correction, or ask for deletion. Contact us and include your Discord user ID. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

8. Security

We keep the Bot’s token and database private and restrict access to the hosting account. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The Bot follows Discord’s age requirements and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe the Bot holds data about a child under the minimum age, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
